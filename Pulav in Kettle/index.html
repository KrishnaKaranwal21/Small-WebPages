Hostel Kettle Pulav

Soya and potato pulav, cooked start to finish in an Agaro multipurpose kettle. One pot, three heat settings, no stove.

Ingredients

Tap an item to tick it off as you gather it. Use the same cup for rice and water.

Main

Spices

To finish

Use a steel spoon, never a metal fork or knife, so you don’t scratch the kettle’s base plate.

Method

Tap a step’s number when it’s done. Steps with a wait have a timer.

1

Hydrate the soya chunks

  • Pour 2 cups of water into the kettle, set it to High / Boil and drop in the soya chunks (about 35–40 g, 1 to 1.5 large handfuls).
  • Once boiling, switch to Off and let them soak.
  • Drain, rinse under the tap, squeeze out all the water firmly and keep aside.
5:00
2

Sauté the base

  • Dry the pot. Add 2 to 2.5 tablespoons of ghee and set the dial to Low, or halfway between Low and Mid.
  • Add 1 level teaspoon of jeera and let it crackle for 20–30 seconds.
  • Add 1.5 to 2 teaspoons of ginger-garlic paste and stir gently with the steel spoon until fragrant.
0:45
3

Cook the masala and veggies

  • In a small glass or cup, mix ½ teaspoon turmeric, 1 teaspoon red chilli powder, 1 full sachet of Magic Masala, ½ teaspoon garam masala and approx. 1.25 to 1.5 teaspoons salt with 2 tablespoons of water to make a slurry.
  • Pour the slurry into the kettle. This protects the base plate from burning.
  • Add the chopped tomatoes (2–3 medium-size), diced potato (1 medium or 2 small, in 1 cm cubes) and the squeezed soya chunks.
  • Cook on Low / Mid, stirring now and then, until the tomatoes soften and coat everything in a thick gravy.
2:30
4

Layer the rice and water

  • Add 1.5 cups of unwashed rice (about 200–250 g) evenly on top.
  • Pour in 3 cups of water, measured with the same cup as the rice.
  • Give it one gentle circular stir to mix the salt into the water. Nudge the potatoes and soya towards the base so the rice doesn’t sit directly on the hot plate.
5

Simmer and control the starch

  • Cover with the glass lid and set the dial to High / Boil.
  • With 1.5 cups of unwashed rice the starch foam rises fast, so watch through the glass.
  • As soon as vigorous bubbles reach near the top rim, turn the dial down to Low and crack the lid slightly to vent steam.
  • Simmer gently until the water drops below the rice surface and steam holes form.
9:00
6

Finish with carryover steam

  • Switch the dial to Off and unplug the kettle.
  • Seal the glass lid completely.
  • Don’t open or disturb it. The residual heat steams the rice and potatoes through to a fluffy, non-mushy texture without scorching the element.
13:00

Fluff, dust, serve

Fluff gently with the spoon, dust with chaat masala and eat it hot.
